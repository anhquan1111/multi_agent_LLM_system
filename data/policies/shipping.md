# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options

| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 business days | Free over $50, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $12.99 |
| Same day (select cities) | Ordered before 11:00, arrives by 21:00 | $19.99 |

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. Orders placed after 15:00 are processed the
following business day.

## Order processing

Orders are picked and packed within one business day. You receive a tracking number by email as
soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse. Until that email arrives the order status remains
`processing`.

## Cancelling an order

An order can be cancelled **only while its status is `processing`**. Once the status changes to
`shipped`, cancellation is no longer possible and you must wait for delivery and then request a
return under the returns policy.

Cancelled orders are refunded in full within 3 business days, including any shipping charge paid.

## Delayed or lost parcels

If tracking has not updated for 7 consecutive days, contact support and we will open an
investigation with the carrier. Parcels are declared lost after 21 days from dispatch, at which
point you may choose a full refund or a replacement shipment at no cost.

## Delivery addresses

We ship to residential and business addresses. We cannot deliver to PO boxes for Express or Same
day service. The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is `processing`.

## International shipping

We currently ship domestically only. Orders with an international billing address are accepted, but
the delivery address must be domestic.
