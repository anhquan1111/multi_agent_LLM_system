# Payment

## Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard, and American Express
- Debit cards carrying a Visa or Mastercard logo
- Store credit and gift cards
- Bank transfer for orders above $2,000

We do not accept cash on delivery, cryptocurrency, or personal cheques.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorised when the order is placed and captured when the order ships. If an item goes
out of stock between those two points, the authorisation is released and you are never charged for
it. Released authorisations can take up to 5 business days to disappear from a bank statement,
depending on the issuing bank.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Timing depends on the method:

| Method | Refund appears within |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | 5–7 business days |
| Store credit | Immediately |
| Bank transfer | 7–10 business days |

We cannot refund to a different card from the one used for the purchase. If that card has been
closed, contact support and we will issue store credit instead.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order is held for 48 hours while we retry. After that the order is
cancelled automatically and any reserved stock is released. You are not charged for a cancelled
order.

## Partial refunds

When only part of an order is returned, we refund the item price plus a proportional share of any
shipping paid. The remaining items are unaffected.

## Taxes

Prices shown include sales tax. Tax is refunded in full alongside any item refund.
