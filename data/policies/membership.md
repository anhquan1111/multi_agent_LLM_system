# Membership Tiers

## Tiers and how you reach them

| Tier | Requirement | Annual fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Automatic on first order | Free |
| Gold | $500 spent in a calendar year | Free |
| Platinum | $2,000 spent in a calendar year | Free |

Tier is recalculated on 1 January. A tier earned in one year is retained for the whole of the
following year even if spending drops.

## Benefits by tier

| Benefit | Standard | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free standard shipping | Over $50 | Always | Always |
| Free express shipping | — | Over $100 | Always |
| Free return shipping | — | Yes | Yes |
| Extended return window | 30 days | 45 days | 60 days |
| Priority support queue | — | Yes | Yes |
| Early access to sales | — | 24 hours | 48 hours |

The extended return window applies to change-of-mind returns. The 45-day window for damaged or
defective merchandise applies to every tier including Standard — see `returns.md`.

## Priority support

Gold and Platinum tickets are answered before Standard tickets in the queue. Target first response
is two business days for Standard, one business day for Gold, and four hours for Platinum during
business hours.

## Losing a tier

If spending in the qualifying year falls below the threshold, the tier drops one level on the next
1 January. Platinum does not fall directly to Standard; it steps down to Gold first.

## Membership and refunds

Refunded orders are subtracted from the annual spending total. A refund that drops you below a
threshold does not remove the tier mid-year — recalculation only happens on 1 January.
