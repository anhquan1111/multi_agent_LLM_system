# Privacy

## What we collect

- Account details: name, email address, delivery addresses, phone number
- Order history, including items, amounts, and delivery dates
- Support conversations and any images you attach to a return or warranty claim
- Technical data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited on our store

We do not store full card numbers. Payments are processed by our payment provider, which returns
only the last four digits and the card brand for display in your order history.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Order records | 7 years, required for tax purposes |
| Support conversations | 24 months |
| Return and warranty photographs | 12 months after the case closes |
| Technical and analytics data | 14 months |

## Sharing

We share data only with carriers for delivery, our payment provider for processing, and tax
authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal data to third parties and we do not
share order history with advertisers.

## Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete your account. Send
the request from the email address on the account and we respond within 30 days.

Deleting an account does not delete order records that we are legally required to retain for 7
years. Those records are retained without being linked to a marketing profile.

## Marketing email

Marketing email is opt-in and every message carries an unsubscribe link. Transactional email —
order confirmations, shipping notifications, return instructions — is not marketing and continues
regardless of marketing preferences.

## Cookies

Essential cookies keep you signed in and hold your basket. Analytics cookies are optional and can
be declined without losing any store functionality.
