# Returns and Refunds

## Standard return window

You may return most items within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund. The item must
be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Refunds are issued to
the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the item.

## Damaged or defective merchandise

If an item arrives damaged or stops working within the return window, you may return it within
**45 days** of delivery rather than the standard 30. Photograph the damage before returning the
item — we ask for images when you open the return request. Defective merchandise is refunded in
full including the original shipping charge.

Items that fail after the 45-day window are handled under the manufacturer warranty rather than the
returns policy. See `warranty.md` for coverage lengths and the claim process.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

- Opened software, digital downloads, and activation codes
- Earphones, earbuds, and other in-ear audio products once the hygiene seal is broken
- Gift cards and store credit
- Items marked "Clearance — final sale" on the product page

## Return shipping costs

| Reason for return | Who pays return shipping |
|---|---|
| Item damaged, defective, or incorrect | We do — a prepaid label is emailed to you |
| Changed your mind | You do — deducted from the refund |
| Wrong size ordered (apparel only) | We do, once per order |

Gold and Platinum members receive free return shipping on all returns regardless of reason. See
`membership.md` for tier benefits.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order.
This keeps the new item reserved for you immediately rather than waiting for the return to arrive.

## Starting a return

Open the order in your account and select **Request return**. You will receive return instructions
by email within one business day. Returns sent back without an approved return request may be
refused or delayed.
