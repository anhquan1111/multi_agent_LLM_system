# Warranty

## Coverage length

| Product category | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Laptops and tablets | 24 months |
| Headphones, speakers, audio | 12 months |
| Accessories, cables, chargers | 6 months |
| Refurbished items (any category) | 6 months |

The warranty period starts on the delivery date, not the order date.

## What is covered

Manufacturing defects and component failures under normal use. This includes batteries that fall
below 70% of rated capacity within the warranty period, screens that develop dead pixels, and ports
that stop functioning without physical damage.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, drops, liquid ingress, and cracked screens
- Damage from unauthorised repair or opening the device
- Normal cosmetic wear such as scratches and scuffs
- Consumable parts including ear tips, cables supplied as spares, and carry cases
- Loss or theft

## Warranty versus returns

These are two separate processes and people confuse them often:

- **Within 45 days of delivery**, defective merchandise is handled as a **return** — you get a
  refund. See `returns.md`.
- **After 45 days**, a defective item is a **warranty claim** — we repair or replace it, and no
  refund is issued.

A warranty claim never results in a cash refund. If repair is not possible we ship an equivalent
replacement unit, which may be a refurbished device of the same model.

## Making a claim

Open a support ticket with the order number and a description of the fault. We respond within two
business days with either a prepaid shipping label or instructions for an in-place fix. Turnaround
after we receive the item is typically 10–14 business days.

## Transferability

The warranty follows the item, not the purchaser, so it remains valid if the product is given away
or resold within the coverage period. Proof of the original purchase date is still required.
